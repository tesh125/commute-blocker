Commute Blocker

A Chrome extension that scans your Google Calendar and blocks real commute time before your in-person events — sized to actual transit or driving time.

Commute Blocker looks for calendar events with a location, calculates how long it'll take to get there (transit or driving, your choice), and adds a "Commute" block before each one so your calendar reflects reality instead of looking wide open right before you have to leave.
